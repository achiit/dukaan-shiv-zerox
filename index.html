A shop built after too many wasted trips

In 2026 I opened this shop in Mangalwada. Before that, my uncle used to travel into the city for a single stamp photo or a form. I saw how much time and money that cost the family, and I decided people in our area should not need to do the same.

I sit at the counter myself. If your document is wrong or a form is missing a paper, I tell you before you pay, not after.
